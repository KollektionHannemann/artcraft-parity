ArtCraft Tracker: how ready is each Craft app for real work?

An unofficial community dashboard for the open-source ArtCraft Craft apps. For each app it shows how ready it is for real work compared with its reference product (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, Lightroom, Acrobat, After Effects, InDesign, Figma, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, AutoCAD, Pro Tools), its feature parity, development activity and releases. Data is read from each project's ROADMAP.md on GitHub every 30 minutes.

  • PhotoCraft: open-source alternative to Photoshop (image)
  • VectorCraft: open-source alternative to Illustrator (vector)
  • FilmCraft: open-source alternative to Premiere Pro (video)
  • LightCraft: open-source alternative to Lightroom (photo)
  • PdfCraft: open-source alternative to Acrobat Pro (pdf)
  • EffectCraft: open-source alternative to After Effects (motion)
  • DesignCraft: open-source alternative to InDesign (layout)
  • FigCraft: open-source alternative to Figma (interface)
  • WordCraft: open-source alternative to Word (text)
  • SoundCraft: open-source alternative to Pro Tools (audio)
  • DeckCraft: open-source alternative to PowerPoint (presentation)
  • CADCraft: open-source alternative to AutoCAD (cad)
  • GridCraft: open-source alternative to Excel (spreadsheet)

ArtCraft Tracker is an unofficial community project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or reviewed by ArtCraft. App names belong to their owners, and the reference products are trademarks of their respective owners. "Ready for real work" and product parity are each project's own estimates, taken from the ROADMAP.md of its repository under github.com/storytold. Activity and releases come from the repositories' git history and GitHub Releases. None of this is an independent measurement. Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data.
